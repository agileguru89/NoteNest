# NoteNest — next development phase

Recorded 6 November 2026 from the owner’s instructions. These are future ideas, not implemented features or permission to integrate third-party services now.

- An elegant living home study with a gently animated fireplace.
- Click the wall board to approach the working surface; zoom back out into the room.
- A television corner that can open authorized YouTube viewing and other permitted free platforms.
- A bookshelf of useful reading and learning sources.
- A music corner for background listening.
- Seasonal room themes such as Halloween and Christmas.
- Potential optional paid themes/Pro functions or company customization while keeping a useful free core.
- Community-driven macOS host and packaging, with real Mac testing.

First priority remains a reliable lightweight Windows note board and verified Google Sheets archive. Later room features must preserve comfortable performance on the owner’s computer and use the relevant platforms’ supported integration and content terms. No agreement with any platform has been made.
