# Privacy

Updated 6 November 2026.

NoteNest stores notes and settings on your computer first. No publisher note
server, advertising or analytics are included.

If you choose Google sync, note text, category, due date and timestamps are
sent to Google Drive/Sheets; basic account email identifies the connection.
The app requests drive.file for app-created/selected files. Google processes
information under its own policies.

Tokens use Windows DPAPI for your Windows user. Disconnect removes local
token copies, not your spreadsheet or notes. Access can also be revoked in
your Google account. Export a backup before manually deleting local app data.
Your spreadsheet remains in your Drive until you delete or share it.

Audio is local and does not call ElevenLabs. Google setup in v1 uses your own
developer configuration; no shared publicly verified client is included.
